# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want OpenMem to be a welcoming, practical, and respectful project. Participate with good intent, give useful feedback, and make room for people with different backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized conduct, publishing private information, and deliberate disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the project maintainers through the contact listed in the repository profile. Reports will be reviewed fairly and kept as confidential as practical. Maintainers may remove content, limit participation, or take other action needed to keep the project safe.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant.
